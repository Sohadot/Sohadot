How the Escrow Sale Works

Sohadot does not process direct payments on-site. This bundle is sold through a seller-approved, independent escrow closing — the buyer's funds are protected until the domains are transferred, and the seller is protected until funds are secured. The full sequence is documented in the Escrow Closing Protocol.

1

Buyer and seller agree on the asset, scope, and price in writing.

2

An independent escrow transaction is initiated and approved by the seller.

3

Buyer funds escrow with the agreed amount.

4

Seller transfers the domain(s) through the agreed registrar method.

5

Escrow releases funds once the transfer conditions are satisfied.
