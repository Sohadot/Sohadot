The thesis

A city does not run on software alone.

Behind visible commerce is a physical operating layer: equipment that builds, moves, stores, serves, repairs and produces. EquipmentNewYork.com gives that layer a direct commercial identity.

New York is not one equipment use case. It is a concentration of equipment-dependent operations.
The name does not force one business model. It can sit above a focused equipment company or become the umbrella identity for discovery, rental, procurement, supply, dealer access or market intelligence. The strategic value is the breadth of the category combined with a precise geographic market.
