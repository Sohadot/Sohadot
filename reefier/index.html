Operating forms

Four forms the identity can take.

Institutional identity

A name for an organization built around hidden structural intelligence.

Analytical framework

A vocabulary for examining depth, interconnection, and resilience.

Index or instrument

A named method for comparing structural conditions.

Strategic program

A durable identity for research, infrastructure, or systems work.

These are operating forms, not sector restrictions. Reefier is not tied to a single industry.

Asset boundary

What Reefier is presented as — and what it is not.

Reefier is presented as a developed reference identity, not as an industry standard, certification, ranking authority, or claim of external adoption.

The Reefier Index is an instrument defined by Reefier for assessing structural conditions in other systems. Its illustrative application does not grade Reefier itself.
