Published Frameworks

Sohadot builds more than digital properties. It develops and publishes original frameworks and specifications for emerging digital systems — turning conceptual distinctions into documented, citable and applicable reference assets.

What "published framework" means here

A published framework is not a blog post or a marketing page. It is a foundational specification with a fixed version, an open license, and a permanent, DOI-registered archival record — designed to be cited, applied and built upon. Each framework is developed as an open methodology and released as a citable technical note, with a public conceptual interface and an inspectable source repository.

This is where Sohadot's naming and category work becomes structured, referenceable output: original categories turned into reference systems that can be evaluated on the strength of their architecture.

Agent Aptness Framework — AAF 1.0
A task-specific framework for evaluating agent–task correspondence

AAF distinguishes an agent's general capability from its aptness for a defined assignment, and evaluates correspondence across five dimensions — skill, knowledge, tools, environment and reliability — resolving to a fit classification from A0 (Unassessed) to A5 (Demonstrated Fit). Its central rule: critical requirements are non-compensable.
